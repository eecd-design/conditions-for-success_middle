The "Active Assessor" is the person currently making changes to the assessment. In the **Change History** dialog, the "Active Assessor" label shows who made a specific change.&#x20;

For version control when collaborating with others, we recommend selecting "Manage Assessors" on the assessment toolbar, listing all assessors, and then selecting yourself as the "Active Assessor" before you start editing.

*Note: You can also select yourself as the "Active Assessor" when first creating a new assessment.*
