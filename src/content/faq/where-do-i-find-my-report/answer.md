You can access the report for a completed self-assessment from the **Report** page. Once on the **Report** page:

* If you are viewing a self-assessment and its status is marked "Complete" then the report will show automatically.&#x20;
* If you are viewing a self-assessment and its status is marked "In Progress" then you will be prompted to "Change Status" to "Complete" in order to view the report. *(A self-assessment must be marked "Complete" before you can access its report.)*
* If you are not viewing a self-assessment, use "Open Assessment" to select the correct assessment from the list.
