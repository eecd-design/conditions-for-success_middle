## Course Overview

This short course explores the purpose, structure, and developmental nature of the continuums and how they support collaborative reflection, self-assessment, and progress planning in middle level contexts.

## Course Structure and Topics

If you're a middle school team member, the introduction module will give you what you need to get started. It's followed by three modules for educators who facilitate the continuums framework. Whether you're new to the continuums or revisiting them, the introduction module highlights how the continuums can strengthen meaningful dialogue, shared understanding, and growth in your school, bringing the middle level philosophy to life in practice.

* Module 0: Introduction
* Module 1: Preparing to Facilitate the Middle Level Developmental Success Continuums
* Module 2: Facilitating the Middle Level Developmental Success Continuums
* Module 3: Planning for Progress and Sustained Use of the Middle Level Developmental Success Continuums
